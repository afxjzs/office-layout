What's measured vs. assumed
  • Measured by you: room 180″ × 94″ × 96″, all window/door sizes and positions, trim, baseboards, sills, heater size, desk shape and height, futon size.
  • Adjusted: heater's east edge sits right where the desk ends (63¾″ from the SE corner), because the desk fits against it exactly.
  • Assumed: desk sits ¾″ off the south and east walls (the depth of the trim), since a 30″ desk top can't sit flush against the window casing.
  • Assumed: sill horns stick out ¾″ past the casing on each side.
  • Estimated: light switch position (just south of the closet casing, 48″ to center).
  • Estimated: futon position along the wall; ladder bookshelf and printer table size and position. Dashed outlines mark these.
  • Checked: overlaps between pieces, walls, heater, door swings, window sills/aprons/casings, door casings, light switch. Heights count, so a drawer unit under a desk is fine.
  • Not checked: baseboards (¾″), desk legs, curtains, outlets, walking space.
